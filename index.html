Trigonometry

The unit circle draws the sine wave

A point runs round a circle of radius 1. Its height is sin θ and its horizontal position is cos θ. Unroll that height over time and you get a wave.

Key idea

The radius is always 1, so by Pythagoras sin²θ + cos²θ = 1 — for every angle. Watch that last readout: it never moves. And because the point comes back round every 2π, the wave repeats.
